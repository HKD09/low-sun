# LOW SUN: how to play

## Start

1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run `npm start`.
3. Open **http://localhost:5180/** in Google Chrome and click the title screen.

To stop the game's server, press `Ctrl+C` in that Terminal window. (`devserve .` works too if you prefer it; nothing needs the internet.)

## Controls

| Do this | With this |
| --- | --- |
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Look | Mouse |
| Fire | Left click |
| Aim down the sights | Hold right click |
| Reload | `R` (it also reloads by itself when empty) |
| Run | Hold `Shift` |
| Jump | `Space` |
| Use a switch | `E` when the prompt appears |
| Open a valve | Hold `E` |
| Swap rifle and pistol | `Q`, or `1` and `2`, or the mouse wheel |
| Record a clip | `V` to start, `V` again to stop and save |
| Pause, and settings | `Esc` |

The pause screen has the only three settings: mouse sensitivity, volume and quality (Low, Medium, High). Medium is the default.

## Recording a clip to share

Press `V` while playing. A red REC timer appears at the top right. Press `V` again and the clip is saved as an MP4 in Chrome's download folder, named `low-sun-` and the date and time. It holds the game picture at 1280 x 720 and the game's own sound; the objective text and other overlays are left out, with only the crosshair dot drawn in. A minute is about 60 MB, and a recording stops by itself after three minutes. The file is an ordinary video: AirDrop it, or attach it in any chat or mail app.

`clips/low-sun-highlights.mp4` is a 66-second sample cut from a run by the automatic player.

## What to do

You are the caretaker of Skarn Head pumping station. Three things tell you where to go:

- the objective line at the top left;
- a small diamond with a distance, which says "up" or "down" when the place is on another floor;
- an amber light pulsing on the switch, valve or stairs you need. It shows through walls.

A voice on the radio (the line of text at the bottom) tells you why.

1. Walk through the tunnel into the long gallery. A sentry wakes there. The hall door stays locked until it is down.
2. Clear the three sentries in the pump hall, then throw the main switch on the north wall.
3. Go down the stairs by the east wall to the pipe gallery and open the three riser valves: hold `E` on each red handwheel until the bar fills. More sentries wake as you do.
4. Come back up and hold the hall while the pumps prime. A bar at the top of the screen fills over about 75 seconds; sentries keep arriving through the roof lights until it is full. Clear the last of them and the control room unlocks.
5. Climb the stairs on the north wall, cross the walkway and pull the master cut-out in the control room.
6. Bring down the lifter that comes through the big door, then step outside.

Doors and switches are interlocked: you cannot run past a fight.

## Weapons

- **Rifle.** Bolt-action: one heavy shot, then it works the bolt for about a second. Five rounds. One hit brings a sentry down.
- **Pistol.** Eight rounds, fires as fast as you click, half the punch. Good when a sentry is close or the rifle is empty; swapping is quicker than reloading. The lifter's plate stops most of a pistol round, so use the rifle on it.

## The machines

- **Armoured sentries** are a third larger than the rest and take three rifle rounds, not one. They arrive during the hold, and one guards the control room.
- **Sentries** always warn you: beeps that quicken, a thin red line, and an amber arc at the edge of the screen pointing at the one about to fire. That takes about a second. Move sideways and the shot misses, or shoot first.
- **The lifter** has a bar at the top of the screen in three parts. Its eye is the weak spot, but only while it glows to fire: a hit there does double damage and spoils its shot, so the moment it aims at you is the moment to shoot. Each third you take off breaks a rotor and brings sentries to help it. After the first, it also sweeps a beam across the room: get behind a pump or a pillar.

If you are hurt, the screen reddens; get out of sight for a few seconds and you recover. If you go down, you restart from the last objective, not from the beginning.

A first play should take about ten minutes.
